# Trak Pilot User Handbook

Welcome to the Trak pilot. This handbook is written for the four kinds of people using Trak
during this pilot: **players**, **coaches**, **parents**, and **academy administrators**. Each
section below is self-contained — read only the one that applies to you.

Trak is a mobile web app. There's nothing to install from an app store; you sign up and use it in
your phone's browser (you can also "add to home screen" for an app-like icon).

---

# For Players

## What you can do

Trak gives you a running record of your football career, built from what your coach logs about
you:

- See every match your coach logs for you, with a result and a performance **band** (from
  *Difficult* up to *Exceptional*).
- See your coach's assessment of you across six categories — Work Rate, Tactical, Attitude,
  Technical, Physical, Coachability — after each session or match.
- Get AI-generated, personalised feedback and drills based on any improvement notes your coach
  leaves you, and ask follow-up questions about how to improve.
- Track your progress over a season on your **Evolution Card** — an OVR (overall rating) built
  from your coach's assessments, with tiers (Bronze → Silver → Gold → Volt → Icon) and challenges
  tied to your position.
- Build a **Player Passport** — a shareable, exportable record of your career totals, season
  history and recognition (awards from your coach), which you can save or share as an image.
- Invite a parent to follow your progress.
- See recognition your coach gives you (Player of the Week/Month/Season, Most Improved, Top
  Scorer) — this shows up on your Passport permanently.

**One important thing to know:** you don't log your own matches, goals, or stats. Your coach logs
matches on your behalf, and their coach view is where ratings come from. This is deliberate — it
keeps the record coach-verified rather than self-reported. There's also no self-tracked "goals"
feature in Trak right now.

A **character/values feature** is planned for a future update — it isn't part of the app yet
(no timeline to share), so don't expect to see it during this pilot.

## Getting started

1. **Sign up.** Go to the Trak sign-up page and choose **Player**. You'll need your name, date of
   birth, nationality, email and a password (at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a
   lowercase letter, a number and a symbol — you'll see the requirement as you type).
2. **Football details.** Enter your position, current club, and age group (U13–U19+), plus your
   shirt number if you have one.
3. **Link to your coach (recommended, optional at signup).** If your coach has given you a code
   like `TRK-AB2K`, enter it here. This is what connects you to their squad so they can log
   matches and assessments for you. If you don't have it yet, you can skip this and link later —
   ask your coach for their code and there'll be a place to enter it after signup.
4. **Invite a parent (optional).** If you want a parent to follow your progress, enter their email
   here — or do it later from your Profile page ("Parent access"). Entering their email creates an
   invite, but **you still need to send them the link yourself** (via text, WhatsApp, whatever you
   use) — nothing emails it automatically. Your Profile page has a "Send link" button that opens
   your phone's share sheet or copies the link for you.
5. **Confirm your email.** Check your inbox for a confirmation link before you can sign in.
6. **You're in.** Once your coach links you to their squad and logs your first match or
   assessment, your Home screen, Evolution Card and Passport will start filling in. Until then,
   you'll see a "Your card is ready" welcome state — that's normal, it just means no matches have
   been logged yet.

Where to find things day to day:
- **Home** — your season band, recent matches, latest coach assessment, and any AI feedback your
  coach's notes have unlocked.
- **Matches** — full match history, filterable by League/Cup/Friendly.
- **Card** — your Evolution Card (OVR, tier, position-based challenges).
- **Profile** — your Passport, parent invite management, and a performance trend chart.

## FAQ / rules

**How is my match rating calculated?**
Your coach logs the match result and (for a full session) details like minutes played, goals,
assists, and cards. Trak's rating engine turns that into a score from 4.0 to 10.0, starting from a
baseline of 6.5 and adjusting for things like the result, minutes played, cards, and
position-specific factors (e.g. clean sheets for goalkeepers, chances created for midfielders).
That score maps to a band: Exceptional (9+), Standout (8+), Good (7+), Steady (6+), Mixed (4+),
Developing (2+), or Difficult (below 2). Your coach's six-category assessment (Work Rate, Tactical,
Attitude, Technical, Physical, Coachability) is separate and comes from direct coach ratings, not
the match-score formula.

**Who can see my data?**
- **Your coach** sees everything they log about you, plus your assessment history.
- **A parent you've invited** can see your match results, season band, coach assessments, and
  awards — but **not** any private note your coach writes for you (see below).
- **Nobody else.** Other players, other coaches, and academy administrators cannot see your
  individual performance data — an academy admin gets read-only, aggregated views of coaches and
  squads, not your personal record.

**Is anything private, just between me and my coach?**
Yes. When a coach adds a written note to an assessment (used to generate your AI feedback), that
note is visible to you and your coach only — it is deliberately not shared with parents.

**Can I log my own matches or goals?**
No — this was a deliberate design decision. Matches and stats come from your coach, so the record
stays consistent and verified. There's no self-logging screen in the app.

**What's the character/values thing I might have heard about?**
It's planned for later — not available in this pilot. No further detail is available yet.

# For Coaches

## What you can do

- Build and manage your **squad** — add players manually, or share your invite code so they link
  themselves.
- **Assess players** on six dimensions (Work Rate, Tactical, Attitude, Technical, Physical,
  Coachability) either one at a time, or via **Quick Assess**, which walks your whole squad fast
  (each slider starts from the player's last score, so you're only moving what's changed).
- **Log matches and training sessions**, either with a 1-minute **Quick match log** (opponent,
  score, who played) or a **Full session** (per-player minutes, goals, assists, cards, and notes).
- Leave **improvement notes** on an assessment — these become the seed for AI-generated feedback
  the player sees, and you can also just talk to your squad about them directly.
- **Recognise players** — Player of the Week / Month / Season (some have cooldowns so they stay
  meaningful), Most Improved, and Top Scorer. Recognition is permanent and shows on the player's
  Passport.
- **Schedule** — a calendar for training and matches, with an option to publish events so players
  see them on their Home screen, plus an AI tool that can read a pasted fixture list or schedule
  text and turn it into calendar events for you to review and save.
- **Coach Assistant** — an AI chat for drill ideas and session plans, aware of your squad and team
  context, that can render pitch diagrams and let you log a suggested session directly.
- View a **squad dashboard**: band distribution, most-improved player, and players who need
  attention.

## Getting started

1. **Sign up** as **Coach**: name, nationality, email, password.
2. **Club details**: your club, the age group/team you coach, and your role (Head Coach, Assistant
   Coach, Academy Coach, or Fitness Coach).
3. **Academy code (optional).** If your academy is running this pilot and gave you a join code
   (e.g. `TRK-XY12`), enter it here so you're visible in their dashboard. You can also join later
   from your Profile page. If the code isn't recognised, signup still succeeds — you'll just see a
   note that you can join later.
4. **Confirm your email**, then sign in.
5. **Get your invite code.** Your Home and Profile pages show your own code, e.g. `TRK-ALEX`. Share
   this with your players — when they enter it (at their signup or later), they land in your
   squad automatically.
6. **Add players manually if you prefer** — Squad → the `+` button — for anyone who isn't signed up
   to Trak yet, or who you don't want to wait on. You can still assess and log matches/sessions for
   manually-added players; only players who've actually signed up and linked (via your code) will
   see any of it on their own account.
7. **Start assessing and logging.** After a match or session, use **Quick match log** for a fast
   result capture, or **Full session** if you want to record who played, minutes, goals, assists
   and cards per player (this feeds each player's match rating). Use **Assess** or **Quick Assess**
   to rate players on the six dimensions.

## FAQ / rules

**What's private, and what do players/parents see?**
- Your six-category assessment scores and match logs are visible to the player (and, if invited,
  their parent).
- Any **written note** you attach to an assessment is visible to the **player only** — not their
  parent. It's also the input the AI feedback tool uses to generate the player's improvement plan.
- Recognition awards are visible to the player and their parent, and live permanently on the
  player's Passport.
- Academy administrators (if your academy uses Trak) get read-only visibility into your squad
  size, assessment activity, and band distribution — they cannot see or edit individual
  assessments, and cannot manage your players directly.

**How does a match rating get calculated?**
You enter the result and (in a Full session) per-player detail — minutes, goals, assists, cards.
Trak's rating engine (starting from a 6.5 baseline) turns that into a 4.0–10.0 score and a band,
factoring in the result, minutes played, cards, and position-specific inputs (e.g. clean sheets
matter for a goalkeeper, chances created for a midfielder). You don't set the number directly —
your inputs drive it.

**Do I need to fill in every field?**
No. Quick match log only needs opponent, score, and who was there — it's built for capturing a
result at full-time in under a minute. Full session gives you more detail if you want it (minutes,
goals, assists, cards, notes) but nothing beyond opponent/score/attendance is required to save.

**What if a player I add hasn't signed up yet?**
You can still add them to your squad, assess them, and log sessions/matches for them — that data
just won't appear on a player-side account until they sign up and link to you with your invite
code (or you match them up later). Nothing is lost by adding them early.

**Is there a manual inside the app?**
Yes — there's a Coach Manual linked from your Profile page with more detail on day-to-day use.
This handbook covers the same ground independently for the pilot.

# For Parents

## What you can do

Once you're linked to your child's account, you can follow their football progress:

- See their **season band** and win/draw/loss record.
- See their **match history** — results, competition, venue, and the performance band for each
  match.
- See their **coach's assessments** — the same six-category scores (Work Rate, Tactical, Attitude,
  Technical, Physical, Coachability) the coach gives your child, with the resulting band.
- See **recognition** their coach has given them (Player of the Week/Month/Season, Most Improved,
  Top Scorer).
- Get **alerts** when a new match is logged, a new coach assessment lands, or your child receives
  recognition.

## Getting started

**You cannot sign up for a parent account directly — you must be invited by your child.** This is
by design: it's how Trak confirms which child's data you should see.

1. **Ask your child to invite you.** From their Trak account, they go to Profile → "Parent access"
   and enter your email. This creates an invite.
2. **Get the link from your child.** Creating the invite does *not* automatically email you
   anything — your child needs to actively send you a link (text, WhatsApp, etc.) using the "Send
   link" button on their end. If you haven't received anything, ask them to send it.
3. **Open the link and create your account.** It looks like `.../parent-invite?token=...`. Your
   email will already be filled in (it's locked to the address your child invited) — just add your
   name and choose a password (at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, lowercase letter,
   number and symbol).
4. **Confirm your email**, then sign in. You should immediately see your child's name and data on
   your Home screen. If you signed up with a different email than the one your child invited, the
   link won't match — ask them to double check the address and re-invite if needed.
5. If you go to the sign-up page directly and pick "Parent" without a link, you'll see a message
   explaining you need an invite from your child — that's expected, not an error.

## FAQ / rules — exactly what you can and can't see, and why

This is enforced by the app's data access rules, not just hidden in the interface — so it's a real
boundary, not a setting anyone can toggle.

**You CAN see:**
- Your child's profile basics (position, club, age group).
- Every match logged for your child — score, competition, venue, and performance band.
- Every coach assessment of your child, across all six categories, with the band.
- Every recognition award your child has received, and any short public note attached to it.
- Alerts summarising new matches, new assessments, and new recognition.

**You CANNOT see:**
- **The coach's private note on an assessment.** When a coach writes a note to help a player
  improve, that note is visible to the player only — never the parent. This is intentional: it's
  meant as direct coach-to-player coaching, not a parent-facing report.
- **Any other player's data.** You only ever see the child (or children) who invited you — there is
  no way to browse other players, teams, or coaches.
- **Anything about a player who hasn't linked you.** If your child hasn't sent you a working invite
  link, your Home screen will say "No child linked yet" rather than showing anyone else's data.

**Why can't I just sign up and search for my child?**
Because the app never lets a parent account discover a child on its own — the link only forms
through an invite your child actively creates and sends you. This protects players: a parent
account (or anyone who created one) can't attach itself to a child without the child's action.

**Is my data / my child's data protected under something like GDPR?**
Trak does have an account-deletion feature (Settings → delete account) that removes your data. That
said: **as of this pilot, there is no separate parental-consent capture flow and no formal privacy
policy or terms of service published in the app.** If you're running or organising a pilot
involving minors, treat this as something to handle outside the app (a signed consent form, a
verbal agreement recorded elsewhere, etc.) rather than assuming the app has it covered — see the
Academy section below for more on this.

# For Academy / Club Administrators

## What you can do

As an academy administrator, you get a **read-only, aggregated view** across every coach who has
joined your academy with your join code — you don't manage individual players directly, and you
can't edit any coach's assessments or squad.

- **Org dashboard** — total players across the academy (counted from every coach's squad roster),
  assessments logged in the last 7 days, and a per-coach breakdown of squad size and band
  distribution.
- **Coaches** — every coach who has joined with your code: their role, team, player count,
  assessments logged this month, and when they were last active. You can copy a coach's individual
  invite code from here (to hand to a player), and you can remove a coach from the academy (their
  Trak account and assessment history are unaffected — they just stop appearing in your dashboard).
- **Squads** — every player across every connected coach, filterable by age group, with their
  latest band and match count. Players whose coach has left the academy are flagged separately as
  "unassigned" so you know to reconnect them.
- **Movement Radar** — players who qualify as ones to watch: average assessment score of 7.5 or
  higher, from at least 2 assessments, within the last 60 days. This threshold is fixed and shown
  on the page — it's not adjustable, and the empty state explains itself if nobody currently
  qualifies.
- **Academy join code** — on your Profile page, share this so coaches can attach themselves to your
  academy (`TRK-XXXX` format).

A **character/values feature** is planned for later — brief mention only, no specifics or timeline
to share during this pilot.

## Getting started

1. **Sign up** as **Administrator**: your name, academy/club name, email and password.
2. **Confirm your email**, then sign in — your academy is created automatically with a unique join
   code.
3. **Find your join code** on your Profile page (`TRK-XXXX`).
4. **Onboard coaches for the pilot:** share your join code with every coach who should appear in
   your dashboard. A coach enters it either at their own signup ("Academy code" field, optional) or
   later from their profile. Until a coach joins with your code, you won't see them or their squad
   at all — visibility is opt-in from the coach's side, by design.
5. **Players are not added by you.** Players join a specific coach's squad (via that coach's own
   invite code), not the academy directly — your Squads view aggregates them automatically once
   their coach is connected to you.
6. Check back on the **Radar** and **dashboard** periodically rather than expecting alerts — there's
   no push-notification layer for admins in this pilot.

## FAQ / rules

**Why can't I add players or coaches directly?**
By design. Visibility into your academy is opt-in — a coach has to actively join with your code,
and a player has to actively join a coach's squad. You cannot add anyone yourself; you also cannot
edit anyone's assessments or squad data. This keeps performance data ownership with the
coach/player relationship and your role strictly observational.

**What exactly does the Radar threshold mean?**
A player appears when their average assessment score (across their six-category coach ratings) is
7.5 or higher, calculated from at least 2 assessments logged in the last 60 days. It resets as
assessments age past 60 days, so a player can drop off the list if they haven't been reassessed
recently — that's expected behaviour, not a bug.

**A coach left — what happens to their players?**
If you remove a coach from the academy, their players' historical assessment data is preserved,
but they're flagged in Squads as "unassigned" until reassigned to another coach in your academy.

**Important — before running a real pilot with minors' data:**
Two things are **not automated in the app today**, and you should plan for them outside Trak if
you're running a pilot involving real players under 18:
- **Billing/payments.** There is no billing or payment feature in the app at all — nothing to
  configure, and no way to charge through the app during this pilot.
- **Formal parental consent.** The app has no built-in terms-of-service acceptance, privacy policy
  display, or parental-consent capture flow. The "parent invite" feature (a child inviting a
  parent to follow their data) is a *feature*, not a *consent mechanism* — it doesn't constitute
  documented parental consent for a minor's data to be processed. If your pilot involves players
  under 18 (which, for this age range, is expected), you should collect and keep consent records
  through your own process (a signed form, a club registration system, an email confirmation
  chain — whatever your organisation already uses) rather than assuming the app has this covered.
